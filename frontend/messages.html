Quick Tips

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drinking water reduces cravings and keeps you healthy. Aim for 8-10 glasses daily.

🏃 Exercise

Physical activity releases endorphins, your body's natural feel-good chemicals.

😴 Sleep Well

Good sleep reduces cravings. Aim for 7-9 hours per night for optimal recovery.

🧘 Meditation

Practice mindfulness to manage stress and urges. Even 5 minutes daily helps!

👥 Find Support

Share your journey with friends or family. Social support increases success rates.

🎯 Track Progress

Log your daily achievements in WellTrack to see how far you've come.
